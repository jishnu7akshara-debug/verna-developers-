Property Liaison for Agriculture Lands

DC Conversion, Change of Land Use (CLU), and Master Plan zonal clearances across BDA, BMRDA, and BMICAPA jurisdictions.

Agricultural Land Conversion Expertise

Converting agricultural land under Section 95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act requires precise synchronization between the Revenue Department (Deputy Commissioner, Assistant Commissioner, Tahsildar) and urban planning authorities. We take care of every legal formality from survey sketches to official conversion order issuance.

Conversion Types & Purposes Permitted:

  • Residential, Commercial & Industrial Conversion: Unlocking full development potential under the Master Plan.
  • Under Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP): Alignment with official zonal regulations and land use mapping.
  • Green Belt Land Under Special Circumstances: Permissible non-agricultural uses in sensitive buffer zones.
  • Farmhouse & Eco-Resort Conversions: Specialized leisure and weekend home permits.
  • Warehouse & Logistics Hub Conversion: Strategically situated alongside National/State Highways and Outer Ring Roads.
  • Convention Halls & Kalyana Mantapas: Meeting mandatory parking, setback, and fire safety provisions.
  • Petrol / Gas Pumps & Fuel Stations: Oil marketing company compliance and explosive department clearance.
  • Agriculture Byproduct & Agro-Processing Units: Food processing, cold storages, and grain storage setups.
  • Change of Land Use (CLU): Reclassification under BDA, BMRDA, and BMICAPA planning schemes.
