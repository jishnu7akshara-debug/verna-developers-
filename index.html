Main Government Authorities & Projects

Most land and property in Bengaluru passes through at least one of these authorities. Over 26 years, we have mastered their processes, archives, and regulations.

Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike

Civic Administration & Local Sanctions

The civic body that runs municipal services and local infrastructure across Greater Bengaluru, managing khata registers, property taxes, and building sanctions.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • A-Khata registration, Khata transfer, and Khata amalgamation across all 198 wards.
  • Property tax assessment, revision of tax calculations, and betterment fee reconciliation.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Bangalore Development Authority

Layout Allotments & Master Plan CDP

The city's main planning authority. It prepares the Comprehensive Development Plan under the Karnataka Town & Country Planning Act and allocates prime layouts.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Allotment of residential and civic amenity (CA) sites, including possession certificates.
  • Execution of lease-cum-sale deeds and absolute sale deed conversion.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Bangalore Metropolitan Region Development Authority

8,005 km² Satellite Growth Zones

An autonomous body set up under the BMRDA Act, 1985. It plans and coordinates development across 8,005 km² including satellite towns and logistics zones.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Land-use change approvals across metropolitan satellite zones and local planning areas.
  • Review and sanction of large-scale residential and industrial layout plans.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Bangalore Mysore Infrastructure Corridor Area Planning Authority

701 km² Township & Highway Corridor

The planning authority for the ~701 km² Bangalore–Mysore Infrastructure Corridor that oversees township clearances, expressways, and buffer zones.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Land-use change within the statutory BMICAPA expressway planning nodes.
  • Township and roadside layout approvals within designated corridor boundaries.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Bengaluru Metro Rail Corporation Limited

Metro Line Alignment & Structural NOCs

A joint venture between the Government of India and the Government of Karnataka responsible for Namma Metro network and statutory structural buffer NOCs.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Procurement of Metro-alignment NOCs for high-rise and commercial developments.
  • Compensation claims, land acquisition advisory, and structural vibration audits.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board

Industrial Land Allotments & Leases

A statutory body formed in 1966 under the Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Act to promote industrial development, allot plots, and administer long-term leases.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Industrial land allotment applications and board approval representation.
  • Issuance of Possession Certificates (PC) and site lease-to-sale regularization.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

City Improvement Trust Board

Historic Bengaluru Title Chains & Allotments

Bengaluru's historic planning trust and predecessor to the BDA. It developed early heritage layouts like Malleswaram, Jayanagar, and Kumara Park.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • Historical title chain trace and verification on CITB heritage layouts.
  • Conversion of CITB lease-cum-sale deeds to permanent absolute sale deeds.
VIEW STATUTORY SCOPE ↗

Real Estate Regulatory Authority (Karnataka RERA)

Developer Compliance & Buyer Protection

Constituted under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016. It regulates and standardizes real estate projects, protecting buyers and investors.

WHAT VERNA HANDLES:
  • RERA project registration assistance and agent regulatory compliance filings.
  • Title due diligence on RERA-registered developments and quarterly reports.
