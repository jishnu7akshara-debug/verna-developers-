About BBMP & Our Liaison Role

The Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike (BBMP) is the apex municipal administrative body responsible for civic infrastructure and property tax administration across Greater Bengaluru. We represent property owners, developers, and corporate buyers to resolve municipal bottlenecks swiftly and legally.

Core BBMP Approvals We Handle:

  • A-Khata Issuance & Title Transfer: Transfer of title in BBMP records following sale deed, gift deed, inheritance (Pouthi Khata), or court decree.
  • Khata Bifurcation & Amalgamation: Dividing parent plots into distinct sub-numbers or combining adjacent plots into a unified property ID (PID).
  • B-Khata Regularization: Assistance with tax-register entries, betterment charges reconciliation, and guidance on conversion to A-Khata.
  • Building Plan Sanction: Floor Area Ratio (FAR) optimization, setback compliance, structural approvals, and fee challan clearance.
  • Occupation Certificate (OC) & Completion Certificate: Departmental site inspections, deviation limit compliance, and final OC clearance.
  • Property Tax Assessments & Arrears Settlement: SAS tax calculation, category correction, and clearing long-standing municipal dues.

BBMP Zones Covered

East Zone, West Zone, South Zone, Bommanahalli, Dasarahalli, Mahadevapura, Rajarajeshwari Nagar, and Yelahanka.
