About BMRCL Metro Buffer Regulations

Bengaluru Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL) operates Namma Metro. Under municipal and state regulations, any high-rise construction, basement excavation, or building redevelopment within designated proximity (typically within 50 to 100 meters) of an elevated viaduct or underground tunnel requires a mandatory No Objection Certificate (NOC) from BMRCL.

BMRCL Approvals Handled:

  • Metro Proximity & Alignment NOC: Procuring official clearance verifying that foundation depth, piling work, and building loads do not jeopardize metro structural integrity.
  • Setback Compliance Audits: Aligning BBMP building plan proposals with mandatory metro right-of-way buffer rules.
  • Metro Land Acquisition Advisory: Assisting property owners affected by metro line widening, station access acquisitions, or viaduct pier footings with claims and compensation tracking.
