PLANNING & ALLOTMENTS

BDA – Bangalore Development Authority

Specialized liaison for BDA site allotments, lease-cum-sale deed regularizations, alternative site claims, denotified land NOCs, and master plan layout sanctions.

About BDA & Our Expertise

Established under the BDA Act 1976, the Bangalore Development Authority is the premier urban planning and development body in Bengaluru. BDA is responsible for formulating the city's Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP) and executing massive residential extensions across the city.

BDA Matters We Handle:

  • Allotment Regularization & Lease-cum-Sale Deeds: Processing delayed lease agreements and obtaining the initial Possession Certificate (PC).
  • Absolute Sale Deed Execution: Final conveyance of absolute title once lease period conditions are fulfilled.
  • Alternative Site Allotment Representation: Representing allottees whose allotted plots were encroached, litigation-stalled, or re-aligned in schemes like Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Layout, Sir M. Visvesvaraya Layout, and Arkavathy Layout.
  • Denotification & Acquisition NOCs: Verifying whether land survey numbers are subject to preliminary or final BDA acquisition notifications, and securing formal Land Acquisition Clearance Certificates.
  • Layout Plan Sanctions: Submitting and regularizing private residential and commercial layout approvals under BDA planning parameters.
  • Change of Land Use (CLU): Processing CLU permissions through the BDA Board for agricultural and green belt parcels.

Notable BDA Layouts Handled

Koramangala, HSR Layout, Banashankari (1st to 6th Stages), JP Nagar, BTM Layout, Sir M. Visvesvaraya Layout, Anjanapura, Arkavathy Layout, and Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Layout.
