Transparent Property Transactions

Unlike volume-driven real estate portals or open-market middlemen, Verna Developers operates as an exclusive transaction advisory. We manage private deals where title perfection, fair pricing, and absolute confidentiality are mandatory.

Asset Classes We Buy & Sell:

Residential Assets

  • Independent House: Legacy bungalows, multi-dwelling houses, and luxury residential estates.
  • Residential Site: BDA-allotted plots, gated layout parcels, and private villa plots.

Commercial & Industrial Assets

  • Commercial Showrooms: High-street retail spaces with maximum frontage and footfall.
  • Commercial Office Spaces: Corporate office floors, independent business parks.
  • Commercial Land & Institutional Land: Land parcels suited for hospitals, educational institutions, or hotels.
  • Industrial Lands & Plots: KIADB industrial layouts, manufacturing zones with heavy infrastructure.
  • Agricultural & Farm Land: Agro estates, plantation lands, and strategic perimeter parcels.
  • Hotels & Resorts: Operating leisure properties, hospitality land, and retreat acreage.
  • Warehouses & Factories: Grade-A logistics centers, manufacturing facilities with clear power sanctions.
  • Other Special Category Properties: Heritage parcels, development rights (TDR), and joint venture tracts.
