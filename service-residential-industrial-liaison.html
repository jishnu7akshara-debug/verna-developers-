Core Specialization

Property Liaison for Residential & Industrial Lands

End-to-end liaison with BDA, CITB, KIADB, BBMP, and Revenue authorities. Clearing stalled deeds, obtaining statutory certificates, and resolving departmental disputes.

What We Deliver

Bengaluru land administration is renowned for intricate paperwork, inter-departmental dependencies, and bureaucratic hurdles. Since 2000, Verna Developers has represented allottees, buyers, industrial corporations, and families, ensuring their applications move smoothly through every statutory desk.

Statutory Clearances Handled

  • Absolute Sale Deed: Regularization and execution of final title conveyance from developmental bodies.
  • Legal Opinion & Registration Services: Thorough search reports, title deeds verification, and registration at Sub-Registrar offices.
  • Allotment of Site & Lease-cum-Sale Deed: Specialized liaison for BDA, CITB, and KIADB industrial plots.
  • Pending Lease Agreements: Resolving historic allotments with unexecuted lease terms or unpaid developmental dues.
  • Possession Certificate (PC): Physical site verification, boundary confirmation, and formal PC issuance.
  • Occupation Certificate (OC): Compliance audit with sanctioned building plans and civic safety norms.
  • Khata & Assessment: A-Khata transfer, bifurcations, amalgamation, and tax assessment clearance.
  • Layout Approval & Plan Sanction: Master plan zonal compliance, development scheme approvals.
  • NOC & Permissions from All Departments: BESCOM, BWSSB, Fire Force, KSPCB, and Airport Authority.
  • Dispute & Court Matter Solutions: We attend any private dispute across various departments and provide civil court solutions.
