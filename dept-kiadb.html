About KIADB Industrial Administration

The Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board (KIADB) is the statutory body establishing industrial estates and allocating industrial plots across Karnataka. Because KIADB plots are subject to lease agreements with production covenants, obtaining clear title or transferring rights requires specialized liaison.

KIADB Matters We Handle:

  • Lease-to-Sale Conversion (Absolute Sale Deed): Transitioning 10-year or 99-year leasehold allotments into freehold absolute sale deeds after project implementation.
  • Transfer of Leasehold Rights: Legal processing of industrial plot transfers, name changes, partner reconstitutions, and corporate mergers.
  • Subleasing NOC & Clearances: Procuring official Board approvals for subleasing surplus industrial sheds or logistics space.
  • Implementation Time Extensions: Resolving show-cause notices for delayed construction or non-implementation of proposed manufacturing activities.
  • Collaboration with KAIDS: Supported by our dedicated sister enterprise, Karnataka Industrial Documentation Services (KAIDS).

Major Industrial Estates Handled

Peenya Industrial Area, Jigani, Bommasandra, Bidadi Industrial Area, Dabaspet, Harohalli, Nelamangala, and Aerospace Park Devanahalli.
