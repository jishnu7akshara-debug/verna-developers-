Legal Excellence

Property Legal Advisory & Court Proceedings

We provide legal opinion, advisory and marketable title for your property for future prospects. We also help you with legal proceedings for your property requirements such as legal notice, Stay/Injunction order, eviction, proclamation.

Comprehensive Legal Guidance & Defense

In Bengaluru's real estate ecosystem, true security begins with unimpeachable title due diligence. Led by co-founder Tejas G. Vernekar, our legal advisory practice protects purchasers, inherited property owners, and institutional developers against historical defects, fraudulent conveyances, and third-party claims.

Legal Scopes & Court Proceedings Handled:

  • Legal Opinion & Marketable Title: Detailed 30-to-50-year title search investigating root of title, original grant orders, registered deeds, mutation entries, and RTC genealogy.
  • Formal Legal Notices: Drafting and issuing statutory notices for breach of contract, specific performance, boundary trespass, and recovery of possession.
  • Stay & Injunction Orders: Urgent civil court representation (City Civil Court / Rural Courts) to secure ex-parte temporary injunctions and status-quo protections.
  • Eviction Proceedings: Lawful tenant eviction suits, unauthorized occupant ejection, and mesne profits determination.
  • Court Proclamations & Execution Petitions: Executing court decrees, bailiff warrants, and formal delivery of warrant (delivery receipt).
  • Revenue Appeals & Revisions: Challenging disputed mutation extracts under Section 136(2) and Section 136(3) of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act before the Assistant Commissioner and Deputy Commissioner.

Document Scrutiny Checklist

  • Original Parent Deeds, Grant Orders, Inam Abolition Orders, CITB/BDA Allotment Letters.
  • Encumbrance Certificates (Form 15 & 16) for minimum 30 consecutive years from the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar.
  • Revenue Records: RTC (Pahani), Mutation Register (MR) extracts, Index of Lands, Akarbandh, and Tippani sketches.
  • Municipal Status: BBMP/BDA Khata Certificate, Khata Extract, up-to-date Tax Paid Receipts.
