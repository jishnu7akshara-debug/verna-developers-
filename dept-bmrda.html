BMRDA – Bangalore Metropolitan Region Development Authority

Regional planning permissions, layout sanctions, and zonal Change of Land Use across Anekal, Hoskote, Nelamangala, Magadi, and Kanakapura corridors.

About BMRDA Regional Jurisdiction

The Bangalore Metropolitan Region Development Authority (BMRDA) coordinates planning for the metropolitan region covering roughly 8,000 sq km spanning Bangalore Urban, Bangalore Rural, and Ramanagara districts. BMRDA regulates regional development outside BDA and BBMP limits through its Local Planning Authorities (LPAs).

BMRDA Services Handled:

  • Change of Land Use (CLU): Reclassifying agricultural zones to residential, industrial, or logistics use under the BMRDA Revised Structure Plan.
  • Residential Layout Approvals: Sanction of plotted layouts, verification of park/civic amenity (CA) relinquishment deeds, and completion orders.
  • Industrial & Commercial Approvals: Clearances for large-scale logistics warehouses, agro-industrial parks, and commercial complexes.
  • LPA Coordination: Direct representation across Anekal Planning Authority (APA), Hoskote Planning Authority (HPA), Nelamangala Planning Authority (NPA), and Magadi Planning Authority (MPA).
