RERA – Real Estate Regulatory Authority

Comprehensive real estate project registration, legal title vetting, promoter compliance filings, and statutory adherence under Karnataka RERA.

About Karnataka RERA & Our Advisory

The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act mandates that all commercial and residential real estate projects where land area exceeds 500 square meters or the number of apartments exceeds eight units must be officially registered with Karnataka RERA prior to any advertisement, marketing, or sale. Verna Developers ensures promoters and buyers maintain 100% legal compliance.

RERA Services Handled:

  • Project Registration (Form A): Preparation of comprehensive legal documentation, encumbrance certificates, development timelines, and project submission.
  • Title Certificate & Search Reports: In-depth advocate certification of marketable title as required by RERA Section 4(2)(l).
  • Quarterly Progress Report (QPR) Filings: Managing mandatory periodic compliance reports, architect certificates, and CA escrow account reporting.
  • Project Extension & Correction Filings: Submitting formal applications for project timeline extensions and sanction plan revisions.
  • Buyer Due Diligence: Auditing registered projects for prospective buyers to verify whether approvals match marketing claims.
