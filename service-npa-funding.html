Capital & Rescue Advisory

Funding on Non Performing Assets (NPAs) & Litigated Property

We facilitate the investment and funding for property requirements in case of emergency, default in banks, litigated property and help you resolve your problem and find the best solution.

Structured Rescue Capital for Distressed Assets

When financial emergencies arise or accounts turn into Non-Performing Assets (NPAs) with scheduled bank auctions under the SARFAESI Act, time is the single most decisive factor. Conventional banks refuse to lend against litigated or distressed collateral. Verna Developers arranges private investment syndication and specialized bridge funding to salvage equity and extinguish debt.

Funding Situations We Address:

  • Bank Default & SARFAESI Rescue: Negotiating One-Time Settlement (OTS) packages with nationalized and private lenders and infusing urgent closing capital.
  • Auction Date Interventions: Mobilizing capital within 48 to 72 hours to prevent distressed distress-sale auctions by debt recovery tribunals (DRT).
  • Litigated Property Liquidity: Funding legal expenses, decree settlements, and stakeholder buyouts to make title marketable.
  • Emergency Financial Requirements: Fast structured funding secured against valuable land, industrial units, or commercial premises.
  • Strategic Asset Turnaround: Partnering with asset owners to clear encumbrances, regularize municipal permissions, and monetize at true market valuation.

Our Process

  • Rapid Appraisal: Immediate evaluation of the collateral value, outstanding liability, and legal encumbrance within 48 hours.
  • Direct Debt Settlement: Funds disbursed directly toward bank loan closure and issuance of No Objection Certificate (NOC).
  • Asset Title Restoration: Restoring the original title deeds to the rightful owners with zero debt overhang.
